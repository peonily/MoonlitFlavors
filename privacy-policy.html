Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025. This policy explains how Moonlit Flavors may collect, use, and protect information when you visit the site.

Information We Collect

Moonlit Flavors may collect non-personal information such as browser type, device information, referring pages, approximate geographic region, and on-site activity. We may also use cookies and similar tracking technologies to remember preferences, understand how visitors use the site, and support advertising.

If you choose to contact us directly, we may receive the information you provide in your message, including your name, email address, and any details you include voluntarily.

How We Use Information

Information collected through Moonlit Flavors may be used to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the site and its content.
  • Measure traffic and understand reader interests and behavior.
  • Display and optimize advertising placements and site monetization.
  • Respond to inquiries, feedback, or support requests.
  • Protect the site against fraud, abuse, or technical misuse.

Cookies and Advertising

Moonlit Flavors may work with third-party advertising partners, including Google AdSense, to display ads. These partners may use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites.

Google, as a third-party vendor, may use the DoubleClick cookie to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website and other sites on the internet. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or YourAdChoices.

Analytics

Moonlit Flavors may use analytics tools, including Google Analytics, in the future to understand traffic patterns, content performance, and user engagement. If implemented, analytics tools may collect information such as pages visited, time spent on pages, traffic sources, browser data, and interactions with site features. This information is used in aggregate to improve the website experience.

For more information about how Google may use data when sites use its services, visit Google's partner sites policy.

Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights related to access, deletion, correction, or restriction of certain personal data. You can also control cookies through your browser settings and opt out of some interest-based advertising through industry opt-out tools.

  • Google ad personalization opt-out: google.com/settings/ads
  • Network Advertising Initiative opt-out: optout.networkadvertising.org
  • Digital Advertising Alliance choices: aboutads.info/choices

Third-Party Links and Services

The site may contain links to third-party websites, including Pinterest, social networks, advertisers, and analytics providers. Moonlit Flavors is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those external services. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before sharing information.

Children's Privacy

Moonlit Flavors is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the site, please contact us so we can review and remove the information if appropriate.

Policy Updates and Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect legal, operational, or advertising changes. Updated versions will be posted on this page with a revised effective date where appropriate.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at hello@moonlitflavors.com.
